Haunted Places in North America

These 17 places in north america are widely reported to be haunted. Ghost stories are folklore and eyewitness accounts, not proven fact. Each page covers the legend, the location on a map and where to read more.

All 17 places

  • Winchester Mystery House – San Jose mansion with staircases leading nowhere.
  • Island of the Dolls – Xochimilco island covered in hanging dolls.
  • Eastern State Penitentiary – Former Philadelphia prison with reported apparitions.
  • Waverly Hills Sanatorium – Kentucky tuberculosis hospital famed for hauntings.
  • Lizzie Borden House – Fall River home of the 1892 murders.
  • Amityville house – Long Island home behind the famous 1970s haunting claims.
  • Stanley Hotel – Colorado hotel that inspired Stephen King's The Shining.
  • Alcatraz – Island prison in San Francisco Bay with ghost tales.
  • Salem – Site of the 1692 witch trials.
  • Queen Mary – Retired ocean liner in Long Beach with reported spirits.
  • Gettysburg Battlefield – Civil War battlefield with many reported sightings.
  • Bell Witch Cave – Tennessee cave tied to the Bell Witch legend.
  • St. Augustine Lighthouse – Florida lighthouse with ghost reports.
  • Whaley House – San Diego house widely considered haunted.
  • Villisca Axe Murder House – Iowa house where eight people were killed in 1912.
  • Banff Springs Hotel – Canadian hotel with a ghostly bride legend.
  • Rose Hall – Jamaican estate tied to the legend of the White Witch.
