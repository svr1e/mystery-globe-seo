Göbekli Tepe

Mysterious place · Türkiye · 37.2200°, 38.9200°

Carved stone pillars dated to about 9500 BCE, older than farming, pottery or writing. It upended the idea that monumental building followed agriculture. Its purpose, and why it was deliberately buried, remain open questions.
