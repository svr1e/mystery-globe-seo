Loch Ness

Mysterious place · Scotland · 57.3200°, -4.4200°

Reports of a creature in the loch date back to a 6th‑century account of St Columba. The famous 1934 “Surgeon’s Photo” was later admitted to be a hoax. A 2018 eDNA survey found no reptile DNA but plenty of eel DNA.
