Nazca Lines

Mysterious place · Peru · -14.7400°, -75.1300°

Enormous geoglyphs of animals, plants and geometric lines scratched into the desert around 500 BCE–500 CE. Many are only recognisable from the air. Theories include astronomical calendars, water rituals and ceremonial pathways.
