Bermuda Triangle

Mysterious place · North Atlantic · 25.0000°, -71.0000°

A loosely defined region between Miami, Bermuda and Puerto Rico where ships and planes are said to vanish. The legend took off in the 1950s–70s, notably after Flight 19, five US Navy bombers lost in 1945. Investigators and insurers note the area isn’t statistically more dangerous than other busy sea lanes; storms and the Gulf Stream explain many losses.
