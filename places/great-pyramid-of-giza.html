Great Pyramid of Giza

Mysterious place · Egypt · 29.9800°, 31.1300°

Built for Pharaoh Khufu around 2560 BCE, it was the tallest human-made structure for nearly 4,000 years. Debate continues about construction ramps and logistics, and in 2017 muon scans revealed a large hidden void above the Grand Gallery.
