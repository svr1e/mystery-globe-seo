Tunguska

Mysterious place · Siberia, Russia · 60.9000°, 101.9000°

On 30 June 1908 an explosion flattened about 2,000 km² of forest, with force estimated at 10–15 megatons. No crater was found. The leading theory is an asteroid or comet fragment exploding in the air.
