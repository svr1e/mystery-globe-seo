Door to Hell (Darvaza)

Mysterious place · Turkmenistan · 40.2500°, 58.4400°

A natural‑gas crater in the Karakum Desert that has burned for decades. The popular story says Soviet geologists ignited it in 1971 to burn off gas; the exact origin is uncertain and records are murky.
