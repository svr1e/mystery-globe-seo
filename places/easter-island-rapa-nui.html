Easter Island (Rapa Nui)

Mysterious place · Chile, Pacific Ocean · -27.1100°, -109.3500°

Home to nearly 900 giant stone moai carved by the Rapa Nui people between roughly 1250 and 1500 CE. How they were moved is debated; oral tradition says they “walked”, and experiments show they can be rocked upright with ropes. The island’s population history is also hotly contested.
