Stonehenge

Mysterious place · Wiltshire, England · 51.1800°, -1.8300°

A ring of standing stones raised around 3000–2000 BCE. Some “bluestones” were hauled about 150 miles from Wales. Why it was built is still debated: a calendar, a burial site, a healing place, or a ceremonial centre aligned to the solstices.
