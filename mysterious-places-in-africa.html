Mysterious Places in Africa

These 14 sites in africa are ancient, unexplained or still debated by historians and scientists. Each page explains what is known, what is disputed and where to read more.

See them on the interactive 3D globe →

All 14 places

  • Great Pyramid of Giza – Built for Pharaoh Khufu around 2560 BCE, it was the tallest human-made structure for nearly 4,000 years. Debate continues about construction ramps…
  • Lalibela – Medieval churches carved down into solid rock in Ethiopia.
  • Great Zimbabwe – Large medieval stone city whose builders were long misattributed.
  • Timbuktu – Fabled desert trading city and centre of ancient manuscripts.
  • Richat Structure – Giant circular formation in the Sahara, called the Eye of the Sahara.
  • Tassili n'Ajjer – Saharan plateau with rock art showing a vanished green Sahara.
  • Bir Tawil – Strip of land between Egypt and Sudan claimed by neither country.
  • Abu Simbel – Temple moved in the 1960s; sunlight reaches its inner sanctum twice a year.
  • Valley of the Kings – Royal tomb valley; the home of the Tutankhamun curse legend.
  • Saqqara – Step pyramid and the hidden underground Serapeum.
  • Mount Sinai – Traditional biblical mountain of the Ten Commandments.
  • Lake Nyos – Crater lake whose 1986 gas release killed about 1,700 people.
  • Skeleton Coast – Namibian coast littered with shipwrecks and bones.
  • Sterkfontein Caves – Fossil-rich caves in the Cradle of Humankind.
